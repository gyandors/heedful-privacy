Heedful: Private Reminders. Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

The short version

Heedful has no accounts and sends nothing anywhere. Your reminders stay on your phone.

What the app stores

The reminders you create (title, notes, date and time, repeat pattern, category, priority and whether they are done), the categories you add, and your settings. All of it is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone.

What the app does not do

Heedful does not collect personal information, does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, does not require an account, and does not request internet access. It does not share data with anyone.

Notifications

Reminders are delivered as notifications scheduled on your phone itself. A notification shows the reminder’s title and notes unless you turn on “Hide reminder details in notifications” in Settings. Depending on your phone’s settings, notifications can appear on the lock screen. Delivery times depend on your phone and are not guaranteed.

Permissions

Notifications: to show your reminders. Alarms and reminders (optional): to ask your phone to deliver reminders at the exact minute. Receive boot completed: to schedule your reminders again after the phone restarts. Vibrate: so notifications can vibrate.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Heedful’s data is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers, so reminders do not move to a new phone. This keeps your reminders on this device only.

Deleting your data

Delete a reminder or category in the app at any time. Uninstalling Heedful deletes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Heedful is a general-purpose app and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app, with a new “last updated” date.
